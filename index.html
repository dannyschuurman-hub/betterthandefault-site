Frequently asked questions

Why can’t the app change the setting for me?

Apple doesn’t allow the app to change system settings. You get exact steps, and the app opens the right screen where Apple allows it.

Is it free?

Yes. Six defaults, the sprint timer and daily tasks stay free.

What does the full version cost? Is it a subscription?

It’s $14.99 once. There’s no subscription.

Does Family Sharing work?

Yes. The full version supports Family Sharing.

Does it work offline? Is my data shared?

Yes, it works fully offline. Everything stays on your phone. The app has no account, tracking or server.

What do the evidence labels mean?

Backed by research means independent studies provide moderate evidence. Small study means one small or pilot study. Our advice, not proven means the advice hasn’t been tested as such.

Is this medical advice?

No. The app gives guidance on phone settings and habits.

When is it in the App Store, and what language is it in?

It’s not in the App Store yet and there’s no release date. Join the waitlist and we send one email when it is. The app is in English.
