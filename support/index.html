Why can’t the app change the setting for me?

Apple does not allow apps to change system settings. The app shows exact steps and opens Settings where permitted.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open a locked setting, tap “See what’s included”, then “Restore purchase”.

Does it work offline?

Yes, fully.

Is my data shared?

No, it stays on your iPhone.

How is the evidence label chosen?

Backed by research
Independent studies with moderate evidence.
Small study

Our advice, not proven
