Why can’t the app change the setting for me?

Better Than Default guides you through changing the setting yourself. When iOS allows it, the app opens the relevant screen in Settings.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open a locked setting and tap “See what’s included”. Then tap “Restore purchase”.

Can I read the instructions offline?

Yes. You can follow the instructions in the app without an internet connection.

Is my data shared?

No, it stays on your iPhone.
