Privacy

16 November 2026

Developer and controller

Danny Schuurman, individual developer, the Netherlands.

Contact: dannyschuurman+betterthandefault@gmail.com

No accounts or connections

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. It makes no network connections of its own.

Stored on your iPhone

Everything (which settings you marked as better, your start score, your tasks, and your rhythm self-check result) is stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your iPhone settings

The app cannot read or change your iPhone settings. It only shows steps and, where Apple allows, opens the right Settings screen.

Purchases

The Full Version is a one-time in-app purchase through Apple, with Family Sharing. Apple processes the payment; the developer receives no name or payment details. The app checks Apple’s signed purchase status on the device.

Brown noise

Brown noise is generated on the device; no audio is recorded.

Your rights

You can email to ask questions. Since the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. You can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

When this policy changes, a new date appears at the top.
