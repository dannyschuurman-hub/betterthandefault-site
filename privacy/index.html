Privacy

Developer and controller

Danny Schuurman, individual developer, the Netherlands.

Contact: dannyschuurman+betterthandefault@gmail.com

No accounts or connections

The app has no user accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or server of its own. It makes no network connections of its own. This applies to the app. The website collects an email address only if you join the waitlist.

Stored on your iPhone

Everything (which settings you marked as better, your start score, your tasks, and your rhythm self-check result) is stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app also deletes the data it stores locally.

Your iPhone settings

The app cannot read or change your iPhone settings. It only shows steps and, where Apple allows, opens the right Settings screen.

Waitlist on this website

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address, your language (English or Dutch), the date and time you signed up and which version of the consent text you agreed to. We use this only to send you one email when Better Than Default is in the App Store. We do not use it for anything else and do not share or sell it.

We do not store your IP address, and the form sets no cookies and uses no analytics. To limit abuse, the server counts sign-ups per hour using a scrambled form of the connection address that expires after one hour.

Where it is kept: on Cloudflare (our hosting provider, acting as processor), in a key-value store. How long: we delete your address right after we have sent the launch email. If the app has not launched within 24 months, we delete the whole list.

Unsubscribe or delete: use the link in our email, or write to hello@betterthandefault.app. We delete it without delay. Basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time, and signing up is optional.

Purchases

The Full Version is a one-time in-app purchase through Apple, with Family Sharing. Apple processes the payment; the developer receives no name or payment details. The app checks Apple’s signed purchase status on the device.

Brown noise

Brown noise is generated on the device; no audio is recorded.

Your rights

The developer does not receive the data you enter in the app. If you email the developer, they receive your email address and any information you include in your message. You can email with questions or requests about your personal data. You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

When this policy changes, a new date appears at the top.
